Collect the right treasures, survive Taliwala, reach the central Portal, and Cross before the ruins claim you.

1Objective & game endReach the Portal and match one revealed inventory requirement. The first successful Traveler is the primary winner. Play continues until every Traveler has either Crossed or died.
2Treasure supplyInventories are shown as Relic, Oddity, and Keepsake counts. Each type has a limited shared supply: 3 each for 1–2P, 6 each for 3–4P, and 9 each for 5–6P.

3Your turnRoll → Move → Act → Portal, if entered. Every active Traveler completes one turn. Then all summoned Monsters resolve automatically in the Monster Phase.
4MovementRoll 1, 2, or 3. Every reachable legal hex is selectable, from an adjacent step through every intermediate distance to the maximum. White outlines are adjacent one-step choices. Gold outlines are route shortcuts; selecting one follows the displayed legal route automatically—it never teleports. If the chosen destination uses fewer than the remaining steps, movement continues from that hex. Every traversed hex resolves its collectible immediately, so passing through a Shield, Armor, or Rune Die picks it up when legally carried. Purple, Teal, and Grey are playable; black ruins are not. After entering from P1–P6, choose any connected direction. Every Traveler and Monster must occupy a different hex, and routes cannot pass through occupied hexes. Stop early only when no legal continuation exists.
⬡ Rune Die · ExperimentalSix equidistant Rune Shrines stand beside the perimeter wall—two on every network. Entering or passing through one bonds its Rune permanently and adds a third die beginning next turn. Maximum one Rune per Traveler. Its faces are Movement ×2, Movement ×3, Soul Exchange, Grand Plunder, Riftwalk, and Wild Surge. Exchange swaps positions with any active Traveler. Grand Plunder targets one Traveler and steals up to 3 total treasures, selected individually, while respecting the 3-3-3 inventory limit. Riftwalk teleports to any open hex matching your network. Wild Surge invokes any other Rune power, then deals 1 protected damage. Runes cannot affect the Major Monster, Answer-or-Die, or Reckoning. On Crossing or death, the Rune Die returns to the same perimeter shrine where it was collected.

5TAKE · GIVE · STEALTAKE: gain one available treasure; optional when a legal treasure exists. GIVE: transfer one treasure; mandatory if legal. STEAL: take one treasure; mandatory if legal. GIVE and STEAL may target only another active Traveler on the same color network. The chosen treasure visibly travels between the supply and character portraits before inventories update. If the rolled action has no legal choice, it skips automatically and the turn continues or ends.
6Entering the PortalThe Portal can be entered only as your exact final movement step. Finish the rolled action, then reveal three Portal requirements. If your sorted three-number inventory matches any one card, you Cross. All 20 possible sorted combinations from 0-0-0 through 3-3-3 appear in the deck.
◆ Resolve · maximum 3Spend 1 Resolve to reroll Movement before moving, reroll the Action die, or replace one revealed Portal card. Each Traveler begins with 1 Resolve. A Portal rejection restores 1, up to 3. After a Portal replacement resolves—or after rejection—the result closes automatically and the Traveler’s turn ends.
7Portal Rejection Escalation · TestingA rejected Traveler returns to their outer entry while keeping treasure. Rejections 1–3 summon M1, M2, then M3. Rejections 4–6 permanently double M1, M2, then M3 movement. Rejection 7 summons a Major Monster beyond the normal 3-Monster cap. Its three dice are Movement 1–6, Multiplier ×2/×3, and Random Path/Teleport. Random Path changes direction every step. Teleport jumps to a random open, non-adjacent hex at the rolled movement distance when possible. When the Major Monster passes through multiple Travelers in one activation, all of them answer the same timed Answer-or-Die question; answers stay hidden and resolve simultaneously. The draining line gives 12 seconds. Correct survives; wrong or timeout means instant death, ignoring Hearts, Armor, and Shield. Judgment closes automatically after resolving. A separate landing or adjacency encounter uses its own judgment.Rejections: 0 · Next: summon M1
8Monster PhaseAfter all Travelers act, each Monster rolls Movement 1–3, Direction A–F, and Attack. Direction matches its six hex-base sides. If movement reaches black, the Portal, or an outer wall, it bounces in the opposite direction. Only 1 of 6 Attack faces triggers a color-network attack.
9Monster damageTravelers begin with 2 Hearts. If a Monster runs through a Traveler’s hex, it deals 2 collision damage and continues to the next open hex; it never stops on the Traveler. Ending adjacent to a Monster deals 1 damage. A rare Attack result deals 1 damage to every Traveler on the Monster’s current color network.
✦ Last BreathThe first time a small Monster would kill a Traveler, that Traveler receives one timed typing challenge drawn without repetition from 100 unique sentences. Type the sentence exactly before 12 seconds expire to survive with 1 Heart. Failure means death. Each Traveler receives only one Last Breath per game. CPU Travelers have a 60% survival chance. Major Monster deaths ignore Last Breath.
10Shield & ArmorEntering or passing through an equipment hex collects it automatically when it can be carried. Carry at most 1 Shield + 1 Armor. A Shield absorbs 1 damage and breaks. Armor raises Heart capacity from 2 to 3. Equipment carried by a dead Traveler drops on that hex.
ShieldArmor
11Crossing, death & supplyOn a successful Crossing, all carried treasure returns to the shared stacks and that Traveler leaves play. On death, carried treasure also returns to supply; Shield and Armor remain in Taliwala on the death hex.
ReckoningThe Portal deck contains one Reckoning card. Once drawn, it is permanently removed and cannot appear again during that game. It resolves one shared requirement across all active Travelers instead of the normal three-card opportunity: matching Travelers Cross; all others are rejected. Reckoning immediately summons the Major Monster, regardless of the rejection count. If it is already active, no duplicate is created. Reckoning is final and cannot be replaced with Resolve.
✦ Living Ruins spectacleRoutes carry character-colored energy, every footstep wakes the stone, the Portal bends nearby light, and important moments receive camera and impact emphasis. Balanced inventories of at least 6 treasures become Resonant. Rare spectral ruin events are atmospheric only. Use Replay last moment on the board to revisit the latest major highlight. Performance mode automatically simplifies these effects.

Tip: build a balanced inventory, watch the shared supply, and decide whether Resolve is worth saving for the Portal.
